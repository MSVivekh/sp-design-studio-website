About the Project

Shaghf Plano

Shaghf Plano is a complete interior buildout for a coffee shop and cafe located in a commercial suite on Preston Road in Plano, Texas. The project transforms a raw commercial shell into a warm, inviting dining destination that blends contemporary design with Middle Eastern cultural elements.

The scope encompasses the full build-out including a barista station with point-of-sale counter, two distinct dining areas, a commercial kitchen with full equipment, new restroom facilities, and service infrastructure. The design features ring-shaped LED pendant lighting, custom curved banquette seating, Arabic calligraphy wall art, and a sophisticated warm palette of oak, cream, and gold tones.

S & P Design Studio provided complete architectural services from concept through construction documents, including proposed floor plans, kitchen equipment plans, elevations, and finish schedules for permit submission.
