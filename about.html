About Us

S & P Design Studio

Our Story

Architecture & Interior Design Studio

S & P Design Studio LLC is a full-service architectural and interior design firm based in Houston, Texas. We specialize in creating thoughtful, functional, and visually compelling spaces for both residential and commercial clients.


Our team of experienced architects and designers brings a diverse range of expertise to every project. We work collaboratively with our clients, understanding their unique needs and translating them into spaces that exceed expectations.


From concept development through construction administration, we provide comprehensive design services that ensure every detail is considered and every vision is realized.

Philosophy

Design That Transforms

We believe that great design should endure beyond trends. Our approach centers on creating spaces that respond to their context, serve their purpose, and inspire those who inhabit them.


The interplay of light, material, and proportion guides our design process. Every project is an opportunity to craft an environment that enhances daily life and stands the test of time.

"Great spaces are not designed, they are discovered through a dialogue between vision and place."
What We Stand For

Core Values

Three principles guide everything we do at S & P Design Studio:

Integrity

We build trust through transparency, honest communication, and a commitment to delivering on every promise we make to our clients.

Innovation

We embrace creative problem-solving and fresh perspectives, integrating modern techniques with time-tested design principles.

Collaboration

We work closely with clients, contractors, and consultants, believing that the best outcomes emerge from open dialogue and shared vision.
