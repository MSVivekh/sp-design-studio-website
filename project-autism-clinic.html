About the Project

Autism Clinic

This project is an interior buildout for Encompass Behavior, LLC, creating a 1,255 square foot autism clinic and therapy center within an existing school building on Grand Mission Boulevard in Richmond, Texas. The clinic is designed to provide a safe, functional, and welcoming environment for children ages 6 months to 12 years receiving behavioral therapy services.

The space includes eight individual therapy rooms, a therapist room, a BCBA office, lobby and reception area, break room, two restrooms, an electrical/janitor room, and a central corridor. Each therapy room features ceiling-mounted cameras, wall-mounted folding tables, VCT tile flooring, and 2'x2' suspended ceiling panels for a clean, distraction-free environment.

The lobby and reception area features wood grain porcelain tile flooring, decorative arched wall elements, and a custom reception desk. The design incorporates ADA-compliant accessibility throughout, including accessible restrooms, drinking fountains, and proper clearances per 2010 ADA standards. Electronic access control with card readers is provided at entry points for security.

S & P Design Studio provided comprehensive architectural documents including site plans, floor plans, ceiling plans, roof plans, finish plans, enlarged plans with interior elevations, building sections, door and window schedules, partition details, signage details, casework details, furniture layout, and power plans.
