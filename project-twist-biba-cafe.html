About the Project

Twist Biba Cafe

Twist Biba Cafe is an interior buildout for a boba tea cafe located along Highway 87 in Crystal Beach, Texas. The compact, efficiently designed space transforms a commercial suite into a vibrant cafe featuring a welcoming seating area, a fully equipped preparation counter, a kitchen, restroom, and storage room.

The seating area features warm wood flooring, built-in banquette seating along the walls, and round cafe tables with Costa dining chairs. A 34-inch pony wall with a glass partition separates the dining from the preparation area. The preparation counter is accented with a bold orange panel and includes boba-specific equipment such as sealing machines, boba shakers, powder and fructose dispensers, a coffee machine, and a snow shaver machine.

The kitchen is outfitted with prep tables, a dual fryer, a freezer, a vent hood, a working bench, a tea brewer machine, an ice machine, and a display cooler. A dedicated storage room provides space for supplies and chemical storage casework. The restroom is ADA-accessible with proper clearances and fixtures.

S & P Design Studio provided architectural drawings, interior design, and construction drawings including floor plans, enlarged plans with interior elevations for every area, and building sections.
