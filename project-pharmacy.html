About the Project

Main Street Pharmacy & Medical Supply

This interior buildout project creates a full-service pharmacy and medical supply store within an existing commercial space in Stafford, Texas. The design carefully separates the front of house retail area for medical supplies from the back of house pharmacy operations, creating an efficient workflow for both customers and staff.

The front of house features an open area pharmacy with retail shelving for medical supplies organized by category including vitamins, supplements, diabetics, personal care, hair care, baby products, first aid, oral care, skin care, and gift items. The space includes suspended wood ceilings, cloud ceiling features, track lighting, pendant lights, and a vertical hydroponic garden as a design accent. A prominent storefront with "Main Street Pharmacy & Medical Supply" signage welcomes customers.

The back of house includes the pharmacy with sorting and dispensing areas, two workstations, will-call shelving, consultation counter with pickup and drop-off windows, a pharmacy supplies room with lockers, a vaccination room, a nutrition room with counseling area, an office, a break room, a customer restroom, a staff restroom, a janitor room, and a storage closet. An overhead door connects the pharmacy to the open retail area.

S & P Design Studio provided comprehensive architectural documents including floor plans, ceiling plans, enlarged plans with interior elevations for every room, building sections, and finish plans with detailed room finish schedules.
