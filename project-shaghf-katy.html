About the Project

Shaghf Katy

Shaghf Katy is an interior renovation project transforming an existing restaurant into a modern coffee shop and cafe on Katy Freeway in Houston, Texas. The design reimagines the space with a moody, sophisticated aesthetic featuring dark tones, copper accents, and rich leather textures.

The scope of work encompasses installing new light fixtures throughout the ceiling and new cove lighting in furring walls, replacing all existing plumbing fixtures in restrooms, new kitchen equipment for cafe operations, complete new flooring and wall finishes in dining areas and restrooms, and two new point-of-sale stations with countertop and casework in the kitchen area. No changes were made to the existing HVAC system design.

The interior features dramatic spiral ring pendant lights, custom curved banquette seating in cognac leather, gold-framed circular mirrors set against textured geometric wall panels, fluted millwork at the barista counter, and Arabic calligraphy branding elements. The overall palette of charcoal, copper, gold, and brown marble creates an upscale cafe atmosphere.

S & P Design Studio provided full architectural drawings, interior design, construction documents, and permit drawings including existing and demo plans, proposed floor plans, ceiling plans, kitchen equipment plans, and dining area elevations.
